# LocalProfi®

**LocalProfi®** is a digital ecosystem that connects customers with trusted local professionals.

Our platform is designed to make finding, choosing, and working with local specialists simpler, more transparent, and more reliable.

## What LocalProfi focuses on

* Connecting customers with verified local professionals
* Professional profiles, portfolios, and reputation
* Job requests, communication, and project management
* Tools for estimates, scheduling, and service workflows
* Building a trusted ecosystem around local services

## Our vision

LocalProfi aims to become the digital infrastructure for local services — bringing customers, professionals, tools, and services together in one ecosystem.
